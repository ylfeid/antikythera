      Method

      Tooth counts and trains come from Freeth et al. 2006 and 2008 for the surviving gearing behind the main drive wheel, and from Freeth et al. 2021 for the Sun, nodes and planets in front of it. 30 of the 69 gears survive; they are drawn in patinated bronze, reconstructions in brass, the speculative Dragon Hand in copper.

      Every coupling is an equation in the angular velocities, solved exactly over the rationals with BigInt; each gear’s mean angle is reduced modulo one turn in exact arithmetic before it becomes a float. The pin-and-slot devices are modelled geometrically and reproduce the Greek epicycle theories exactly. Pointer settings are fitted to the ephemeris over 19 years around the epoch; nothing else is fitted.
